Delete your Phillic account

This public page lets you authenticate directly with Phillic's Supabase account service and permanently delete your Phillic cloud account without reinstalling the app.

Before you delete

  • Deletion permanently removes your Phillic account and the matching RevenueCat customer record. The server deletes RevenueCat data first and deletes the Supabase Auth user only after that succeeds.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel through the store account used to subscribe so future store billing stops.
  • Hydration entries and profile fields are stored only on your devices in this release. The web cannot access or erase them. Delete Phillic or clear its app storage on each device to remove those local records.

Secure deletion

Verify your Phillic account

Your credentials are sent over HTTPS directly to Supabase. They are not stored by this page.

What is deleted and when

After successful verification and confirmation, the deletion normally completes immediately: the Phillic RevenueCat customer is removed first, then the Supabase Auth account. If the first step fails, the Supabase account remains and the page reports an error instead of partially completing the request. Limited security logs, store transaction records, legal records, or isolated backup copies may remain only as described in the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Need help instead?

If you cannot authenticate, contact phillicpro@gmail.com from the email address associated with the account. Do not email your password. Support may need to verify account ownership before acting on a manual request.
