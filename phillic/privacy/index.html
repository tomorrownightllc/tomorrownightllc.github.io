Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Phillic handles information when you use the Phillic mobile application. Phillic is provided by Tomorrow Night LLC.

Effective and last updated: August 24, 2026

Information Phillic handles

Information kept on your device

Phillic stores your hydration entries and app profile locally on your device. Depending on what you choose to enter, the profile can include a display name, email address, age range, gender, height, pregnancy or breastfeeding status, activity level, climate, hydration goal, display preferences, theme, and reminder schedule. Locally scheduled reminder information also stays on your device. Phillic does not upload these hydration entries or profile fields to Tomorrow Night LLC in this release.

Phillic account information

If you create an account, Phillic sends your email address, password, optional display name, and account-session information to Supabase, our authentication provider. Supabase stores a protected password representation rather than the password you typed. Supabase may also process ordinary security and operational information, such as IP address, timestamps, and request metadata, to provide and protect account services.

Subscription information

Apple App Store or Google Play processes your purchase and payment information. Phillic uses RevenueCat to determine whether your Phillic Premium entitlement is active and to support purchase restoration. RevenueCat receives the Phillic account identifier when you are signed in, your email address, app/store identifiers, and subscription or transaction status. Phillic does not receive your full payment-card details.

How information is used

  • Provide account sign-in, confirmation, and password recovery.
  • Provide and restore Phillic Premium access.
  • Protect Phillic, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot account or subscription problems.
  • Respond to support, privacy, or legal requests that you send us.

Phillic does not include third-party advertising or sell your personal information.

When information is shared

Information is shared with Supabase for authentication, RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management, and Apple or Google for store billing. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Local notifications, exports, and external links

Hydration reminders are scheduled locally after you grant notification permission; Phillic does not obtain an Expo push token for these reminders. Clinical reports are generated from data on your device and are shared only when you choose a destination. Phillic also links to third-party research and store pages; those destinations receive the normal technical data associated with a web request and apply their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Account and RevenueCat customer records are kept while your account is active. An authenticated deletion request removes the RevenueCat customer record before deleting the Supabase Auth user. Limited security logs, store transaction records, legal records, or backup copies may be retained only for the period reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or the applicable provider's backup cycle, after which they are deleted or de-identified.

Because hydration history and profile details are stored locally, Tomorrow Night LLC cannot remotely read or erase those device-only records. In-app deletion clears Phillic's local data on that device after server deletion succeeds. If you delete your account on the web, you can clear remaining device-only information by deleting Phillic or clearing its app storage.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to create a Phillic account.
  • You can disable reminders in Phillic or in your device settings.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple App Store or Google Play.
  • You can delete your account in Phillic or through the public deletion page.

Security

Phillic uses HTTPS for account and subscription communications, authenticated server-side deletion, and platform-supported secure session storage. No security measure is perfect, but we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle.

Children

Phillic is not directed to children under 13, and Tomorrow Night LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through Phillic. Contact us if you believe a child has provided account information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Phillic changes. The effective date above will identify the latest version. For privacy questions or requests, email phillicpro@gmail.com.
