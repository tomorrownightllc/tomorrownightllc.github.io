Phillic Support

Help with your Phillic account, subscription access, or app experience.

Contact

Email Phillic support

phillicpro@gmail.com

Include your device type, iOS or Android version, Phillic version, and a short description of what happened. Never send your password or sensitive health information by email.

Account access

Use Reset password in Phillic Settings if you cannot sign in. If you created an account but did not receive a confirmation or recovery email, check spam and confirm that you entered the correct address before contacting support.

Subscriptions

Use Restore purchases in Phillic Settings when an active subscription is not recognized. Purchases, cancellations, billing, and refunds are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play account used for checkout.

Important health notice

Phillic is an informational hydration-tracking tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, monitoring, or treatment. For urgent symptoms or a medical emergency, contact local emergency services. Ask a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical guidance.
